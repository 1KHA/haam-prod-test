Program Manager Dashboard Features
1. Dashboard Overview
View key metrics (Total startups, applications, funding requests, mentorship sessions).
Quick access to pending approvals, upcoming events, and alerts.
Performance analytics on startup progress, engagement, and funding.
2. Startup & Cohort Management
Cohort Creation & Management

Create and manage multiple cohorts (e.g., Batch 2025, FinTech Accelerator).
Assign startups to specific cohorts.
Track overall progress of each cohort.
Startup Profiles

Access detailed profiles of each startup (Industry, Team, Revenue, Milestones).
Monitor key metrics like traction, funding stage, and product updates.
Upload and manage reports related to each startup.
Milestone & KPI Tracking

Define required milestones for startups (Product launch, customer acquisition).
Track real-time progress on business goals.
Generate reports on startup performance.
3. Application Management
Application Review & Evaluation

Review startup applications for accelerator programs.
Assign reviewers to evaluate applications.
Score and provide feedback on applications.
Shortlisting & Selection

Filter and shortlist applications based on predefined criteria.
Approve/reject applications and send notifications.
Manage interview schedules for shortlisted startups.
4. Mentorship & Advisor Coordination
Mentor Assignments

Assign mentors to startups based on industry and expertise.
Track mentor-startup interactions.
Review mentor feedback and recommendations.
Session Scheduling & Tracking

Approve or schedule mentorship sessions.
Monitor attendance and feedback from both startups and mentors.
Maintain logs of all mentorship activities.
5. Funding & Investment Management
Funding Request Approvals

Review and approve startup funding requests.
Validate financial justifications and business use cases.
Track fund disbursement history.
Investor Relations

Connect startups with potential investors.
Track investor interest and startup pitches.
Manage demo day participation and investor interactions.
Grant & Equity Management

Track grant disbursements and compliance.
Monitor equity stakes in funded startups.
Generate funding reports for stakeholders.
6. Learning & Resource Hub
Upload and manage learning materials (Videos, PDFs, Guides).
Track startup engagement with educational resources.
Provide curated content recommendations based on startup stage.
7. Event & Workshop Management
Event Planning & Scheduling

Create and manage accelerator events (Demo Day, Workshops).
Send invites and track RSVPs.
Manage post-event feedback.
Speaker & Partner Management

Invite industry experts for guest sessions.
Coordinate logistics for panel discussions and networking sessions.
Monitor event success metrics.
8. Communication & Notifications
Send announcements and program updates to startups.
Automate reminders for deadlines, mentorship sessions, and reports.
Provide a messaging system for startups to contact program managers.
9. Reports & Analytics
Startup & Cohort Performance Reports

Generate reports on startup growth, traction, and funding.
Compare different cohorts’ performance.
Financial & Funding Reports

Track overall funding disbursement across cohorts.
View investor interest trends.
Engagement Metrics

Monitor mentorship session effectiveness.
Track startup participation in events and training.
