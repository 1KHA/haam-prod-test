Judge Dashboard Features
1. Dashboard Overview
Quick Access to Assigned Tasks: View upcoming evaluations, pitch events, and pending applications.
Real-Time Scoring Updates: Track startups evaluated, average scores, and ranking.
Notifications & Deadlines: Reminders for upcoming judging sessions, score submission deadlines, and feedback requests.
2. Profile & Availability Management
Profile Setup: Update bio, expertise, and judging preferences (e.g., industry focus).
Availability Scheduling: Set available time slots for reviewing applications or attending pitch sessions.
3. Application & Pitch Evaluation
Application Review & Scoring

Access startup applications with business summaries, pitch decks, and financial projections.
Score startups based on predefined criteria (e.g., Market Viability, Team Strength, Scalability).
Submit comments and recommendations.
Pitch Competition Scoring

View live or recorded startup pitches.
Score based on criteria like presentation clarity, business potential, and innovation.
Compare scores with other judges for ranking.
Feedback Submission

Provide structured feedback on strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
Option to leave confidential comments for program managers.
4. Startup Shortlisting & Decision Making
Final Evaluation Summary: See a compiled list of top-ranked startups based on judge scores.
Collaborative Discussion: Engage in discussions with other judges for final selections.
Voting & Approval System: Vote on startups for funding, awards, or program admission.
5. Event & Demo Day Participation
Judge Startup Pitches: Attend or review recorded demo day pitches.
Provide Live Feedback: Submit instant feedback during pitch events.
Track Event Schedule: View assigned judging sessions and event agenda.
6. Reports & Insights
Judging Performance Analytics: Track startups evaluated, average scores given, and feedback impact.
Startup Growth Tracking: Follow progress of judged startups over time.
Final Reports: Download and review overall judging results.
