Mentor Dashboard Features
1. Dashboard Overview
Quick summary of assigned startups.
Notifications for upcoming mentorship sessions.
Quick access to feedback requests, progress updates, and reports.
KPIs tracking mentorship impact (session completion rate, startup feedback).
2. Profile & Availability Management
Profile Management

Update professional details (Bio, Industry Expertise, Achievements).
Set preferred communication methods (Email, Video Calls, In-Person).
Upload resources (Presentation decks, Templates, Case Studies).
Availability & Scheduling

Set available time slots for mentorship.
Approve or reschedule startup session requests.
Sync calendar with Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.
3. Startup Mentorship Management
Startup Assignments

View a list of assigned startups.
Access startup profiles (Industry, Business Model, Traction).
Track startup progress and milestones.
Session Scheduling & Tracking

View scheduled sessions and past meetings.
Accept, decline, or reschedule mentorship requests.
Track attendance and engagement.
One-on-One & Group Sessions

Host individual or group mentorship sessions.
Assign pre-session tasks or reading materials.
Provide session summaries and key takeaways.
4. Feedback & Progress Tracking
Post-Session Feedback

Provide feedback on startup progress and challenges.
Rate startup performance in key areas (Product Development, Market Fit).
Assign action items for the next session.
Milestone & KPI Tracking

Monitor startup milestone progress (Funding, Revenue Growth).
Provide recommendations to refine startup strategies.
Flag startups at risk of stagnation.
Surveys & Reports

Fill out structured feedback forms after mentorship sessions.
Access startup-generated reports and updates.
5. Communication & Collaboration
Messaging & Chat

Direct messaging with assigned startups.
Group discussions with startup founders and team members.
File sharing for resources and guidance.
Discussion Forums & Peer Collaboration

Participate in mentor-exclusive forums.
Exchange insights with other mentors.
Share industry trends and best practices.
6. Resource Sharing & Learning Hub
Mentor-Contributed Resources

Upload guides, case studies, and templates.
Share best practices for startup growth.
Access to Accelerator’s Learning Materials

View program curriculum to align mentorship with program goals.
Access industry reports and research papers.
7. Event & Demo Day Participation
Attend Events & Workshops

Register for accelerator-hosted events.
Participate in startup pitch days and panel discussions.
Judge Startup Pitches

Provide feedback on pitch decks.
Score startup presentations for investor readiness.
8. Performance & Impact Reports
Mentorship Impact Analytics

View engagement metrics (Number of sessions, Feedback scores).
Track startup progress against initial benchmarks.
Startup Growth Reports

Receive updates on startup revenue, funding, and traction.
Analyze impact of mentorship on startup success.
