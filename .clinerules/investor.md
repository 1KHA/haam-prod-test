Investor Dashboard Features
1. Dashboard Overview
Investment Portfolio Summary: View total investments, ROI, and active startups.
Upcoming Events & Pitch Days: See scheduled demo days and investor meetings.
Notifications & Updates: Get alerts on new investment opportunities, startup progress, and funding rounds.
2. Investor Profile Management
Personalized Profile: Update investor details, focus areas, and investment preferences.
Fund Type & Strategy: Define investment strategy (Angel, VC, Grant Provider).
Visibility Settings: Choose whether to be publicly visible to startups or engage privately.
3. Startup Discovery & Due Diligence
Browse Startups:

Filter startups based on industry, stage (Pre-seed, Series A, etc.), traction, and funding needs.
View startup profiles, pitch decks, and key business metrics.
Save startups to a watchlist for further review.
Advanced Search & Filtering:

Search startups by valuation, funding goals, revenue, or team size.
Compare multiple startups side by side.
Investment Readiness Reports:

Access due diligence reports prepared by the accelerator.
View mentor feedback and milestone progress.
Track legal and compliance status of startups.
4. Pitch & Investment Management
Demo Day & Live Pitch Viewing:

Join live startup pitch sessions.
Review recorded pitch videos.
Submit real-time feedback.
One-on-One Meetings with Startups:

Request meetings with startup founders.
Schedule discussions on business model, growth strategy, and funding needs.
Investment Interest & Commitments:

Express interest in funding startups.
Pledge investment amounts and define deal terms.
Track co-investment opportunities with other investors.
5. Deal Flow & Funding Management
Investment Agreement Tracking:

Review and sign term sheets, SAFE notes, or equity agreements.
Access legal templates for funding deals.
Track progress of investment disbursements.
Portfolio Management:

Monitor investment performance across different startups.
View financial reports and key growth indicators.
Get alerts on follow-on funding rounds.
6. Startup Performance & Exit Tracking
Performance Dashboard:

Track startup KPIs like revenue growth, market traction, and customer acquisition.
Receive quarterly financial reports from funded startups.
Exit & Liquidity Monitoring:

Get notified of acquisition, IPO, or secondary market opportunities.
Access exit strategy discussions and investor updates.
7. Networking & Community Engagement
Investor-Only Forums:

Connect with other investors and share insights.
Discuss emerging trends and co-investment opportunities.
Mentor & Advisor Collaboration:

Engage with startup mentors to understand startup potential.
Join expert panels and knowledge-sharing sessions.
8. Reports & Analytics
Investment Performance Reports:

Get reports on total investments, portfolio health, and expected ROI.
Access trend analysis on startup success rates.
Funding Trends & Market Insights:

Receive accelerator-curated reports on investment opportunities.
Track funding trends by sector and geography.
